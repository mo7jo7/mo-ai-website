About us

Making AI approachable, one video at a time

LiteAI Lab is a YouTube channel for curious people who want to understand and use AI in a practical, lightweight way.

Our mission

Clear, practical and hype-free

AI moves quickly, and it is easy to feel lost. Our mission is to cut through the noise with explanations that are simple, honest and useful, so you can learn by doing.

We favour lightweight approaches: tools and techniques that are easy to try, easy to understand and easy to adapt to your own projects.

Visit the channel

What we cover

What you will learn

  • How modern AI tools work, explained without jargon.
  • Step-by-step tutorials you can follow and repeat.
  • Simple experiments that show what AI can and cannot do.
  • Useful tools and workflows to save time on real tasks.
  • How to compare options and choose what fits your needs.

Content categories

Four kinds of videos

Every video fits one or more of these categories. Use the tag filters on the home page to find them.

Tutorials

Guided, step-by-step lessons that take you from a clear starting point to a working result.

Experiments

Hands-on tests and comparisons that explore how AI behaves, with results you can reproduce.

Tools

Walkthroughs of helpful apps, libraries and workflows, and how to fit them into your day.

Reviews

Balanced reviews that look at strengths, limits and who each tool is really for.
