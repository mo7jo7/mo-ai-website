FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can we collaborate on a video or project?

We are open to collaborations that fit the channel's focus on practical, lightweight AI. Choose “Collaboration” in the form and tell us a little about your idea.

Do you accept sponsorships?

We consider sponsorships that are relevant and useful to our viewers. Send the details through the form using the “Sponsorship” topic. Sponsored content is always clearly disclosed.

Can I request a video topic?

Yes, requests are welcome. Use the “Video request” topic or comment on a recent video. We cannot promise every topic, but we read them all.

How long does a reply take?

We aim to reply as soon as we can. Response times vary, so please be patient if it takes a few days.

Do you offer one-to-one help or consulting?

The channel is focused on public, shared learning, so we generally do not offer individual support. The videos and playlists are the best place to start.
